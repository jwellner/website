## About

Hi, I'm Jeroen. I'm a freelance software/devops engineer (**Wellner IT Consultancy**), building things for the internet since 1996.

These days that means shipping LLM features into production products, picking and evaluating the models behind them, and running a team of AI coding agents alongside my own work.

I also run what I build: when production misbehaves (slow queries, failing alerts, mail that doesn't arrive) I'm usually the one who finds out why, and I make sure releases ship safely.
